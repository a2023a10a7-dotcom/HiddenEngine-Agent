# Visual direction — THE FALL OF NVIDIA'S KING
**Status:** Shot plan only. No footage has yet been acquired or rights-cleared.

## Overall edit language
A premium business-investigation documentary. Open on black, restrained low-frequency sound, short flashes of verified archival imagery, then a measured pace. Use the existing near-black / ivory / muted-copper identity. Do not overuse zooms on stills. Alternate real moving footage, archival stills, official documents, clean typography, maps and data graphics.

## Scene plan
1. **Cold open:** Verified Jensen Huang keynote footage or official NVIDIA event footage; NVIDIA campus exterior; a clean date card “NOVEMBER 2026”; cut to the official SEC filing. Do not use AI-generated likeness footage.
2. **The official break:** Official NVIDIA CEO transition announcement; SEC Form 8-K full-screen document treatment; Colette Kress official portrait; Harvey Jones official portrait; restrained timeline from Nov 11 to Nov 17.
3. **The crisis:** Use only licensed/authorized portraits and dated news stills; display NVIDIA's Nov 8 board statement as a document; use neutral abstract graphics for “reported / investigated / not established”. Avoid illustrative footage that implies a real clandestine meeting.
4. **Why the chips matter:** Close-ups of NVIDIA GPUs and server systems from official media; data-center footage; export-control timeline and map only after official government source review. Label illustrative chip imagery accurately.
5. **The return that did not happen:** Huang's actual public statement if a canonical source can be retrieved; board-chair statement from earnings call; timeline; Reuters fact-check document showing the fake letter was fabricated and the genuine open letter is separate.
6. **The company beneath the founder:** NVIDIA earnings release, verified revenue chart, data-center segment chart, supply-chain / manufacturing footage with accurate source captions. Do not imply all manufacturing occurs at NVIDIA-owned fabs.
7. **What happens next:** Official House committee announcement and scheduled interview dates; Kress official portrait; empty keynote stage / company exterior; close on NVIDIA logo with a question-led end card.

## Asset sourcing priorities
1. NVIDIA newsroom / investor relations media — verify usage terms for each asset.
2. SEC EDGAR filings — public regulatory documents; use as document evidence.
3. U.S. House committee official pages and public-domain government imagery where applicable.
4. Wikimedia Commons — check each file's specific license and attribution.
5. Pexels / Pixabay — only for generic illustrative data-center or chip-factory footage; never represent it as NVIDIA or a named event.
6. AP / Reuters / Getty images — do not use unless a license is explicitly obtained.

## Edit requirements
- Target 9–11 minutes; use purposeful shot changes, not a slideshow.
- Keep most b-roll shots 3–7 seconds, with longer holds for documents and key statements.
- Use timeline graphics for dated events; no invented dates.
- Use split-screen only to compare documented statements.
- Include lower-thirds for attribution and “ALLEGATION / INVESTIGATION / NOT ESTABLISHED” where needed.
- Use restrained sound design; avoid ominous spy-movie cues that imply guilt.
- Every visual asset needs a source URL, creator, exact license, access date, and attribution note before final export.
