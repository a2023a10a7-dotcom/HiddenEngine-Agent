# Documentary production brief

**Topic:** NVIDIA: How It Became the Engine of the AI Infrastructure Boom  
**Project:** 2026-11-14-nvidia-hidden-engine  
**Target duration:** 15 minutes  
**Status:** TOPIC RECEIVED — RESEARCH REQUIRED

## Editorial direction

Explain the economic and strategic mechanism behind NVIDIA's rise: not simply "AI is growing", but how a GPU company assembled a hardware, software, networking, systems, and developer platform that captures value from the AI infrastructure buildout.

## Non-negotiable evidence rules

- No factual narration until each material claim has a source record.
- Prefer SEC filings, annual reports, official technical documentation, original datasets, regulatory records, and independent technical research.
- Distinguish reported fact, attributed statement, estimate, analysis, disputed claim, and speculation.
- A search result or repeated headline is a lead, not corroboration.
- Do not treat NVIDIA's forecasts or management statements as independently verified outcomes.
- Never use illustrative footage as if it depicts the actual company, person, product, or event.
- Record asset provenance, license, permitted use, and attribution before edit approval.
- Separate customer headquarters revenue from end-customer demand and shipment destination where the filings make that distinction.

## Core investigative questions

1. What is the actual source of NVIDIA's revenue and operating profit?
2. What is the role of CUDA and the wider software ecosystem in customer switching costs?
3. How much of the platform depends on external foundries, advanced packaging, memory, and networking?
4. Who pays for AI infrastructure, and what returns must those customers generate?
5. How concentrated are customers and receivables?
6. What are the competitive and geopolitical limits to growth?

## Scene architecture

- **S01 — Cold open** (63s): Open with a verified contradiction, consequence, or striking documented fact.
- **S02 — The system** (135s): Explain NVIDIA's business model, GPU platforms, customer groups, and ecosystem.
- **S03 — The hidden engine** (198s): Reveal revenue mechanics, margins, supply chain, software leverage, and infrastructure economics.
- **S04 — The strategic bet** (180s): Reconstruct documented bets on accelerated computing, CUDA, data centers, and platform transitions.
- **S05 — The turning point** (162s): Show what changed through dated primary evidence, from gaming GPUs to AI infrastructure.
- **S06 — Who wins and pays** (108s): Trace customer concentration, capex, energy, manufacturing dependencies, competition, and geopolitical exposure.
- **S07 — What remains** (54s): Close with a balanced view of durability, constraints, and unresolved questions.
